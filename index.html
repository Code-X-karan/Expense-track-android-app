Smart Expense Tracker helps you manage your finances easily. Track your income, monitor expenses, and export reports — all in one simple app.

Whether you're saving for a goal or just keeping tabs on daily spending, Smart Expense Tracker makes it effortless. Add transactions in seconds, view detailed breakdowns, and export reports anytime you need them.

Built with simplicity and speed in mind — no complicated setup, no unnecessary features. Just clean, effective expense tracking.
